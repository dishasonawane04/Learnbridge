Common Questions

Everything you need to know about Learnbridge

How does the AI process my files?

Our AI uses advanced NLP models to scan your PDFs, documents, or notes. It extracts key concepts, identifies definitions, and maps out relationships to create your personalized learning roadmap.

Can I use it for any subject?

Yes! Learnbridge is subject-agnostic. Whether you're studying STEM, humanities, law, or medicine, the AI can analyze your content and generate relevant study materials tailored to you.

Is my data secure?

Privacy is our priority. Your uploaded materials are encrypted and stored securely. We do not use your personal notes to train public AI models or share your data with third parties.
