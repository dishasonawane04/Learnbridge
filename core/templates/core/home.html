Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI process my files?

Our AI uses advanced NLP models to scan your PDFs, documents, or notes. It extracts key concepts, identifies definitions, and maps out the relationships between different topics to create your personalized learning roadmap.

Can I use it for any subject?

Yes! Learnbridge is designed to be subject-agnostic. Whether you're studying STEM subjects, humanities, or law, the AI can analyze your specific course content and generate relevant study materials.

Is my data secure?

Data privacy is our top priority. Your uploaded materials are encrypted and stored securely. We do not use your personal notes to train public AI models.
